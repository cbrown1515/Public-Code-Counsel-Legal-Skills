# Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (fictional test document)

> **Fictional.** Both companies and every term below are invented for testing the NDA Review skill. Any resemblance to a real company or agreement is accidental.

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Copperfin Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Copperfin"), and Halvard Analytics LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Halvard"). A party disclosing information under this Agreement is the "Disclosing Party," and a party receiving it is the "Receiving Party."

**1. Purpose.** The parties wish to evaluate a potential integration of Copperfin's robotic arm controllers with Halvard's analytics platform, including the controller specifications listed in Exhibit A (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by a Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether written, oral or visual, and whether or not marked as confidential.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was known to the Receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; or (c) is received from a third party who is free to disclose it.

**4. Use and Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and may disclose it only to its employees who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

**5. Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like importance, and no less than reasonable care.

**6. Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is required by law or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall first obtain a protective order covering such Confidential Information before making any disclosure.

**7. Term.** This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date. The Receiving Party's obligations under this Agreement shall survive for five (5) years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

**8. Return of Information.** Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and all copies, and an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify such destruction in writing.

**9. Non-Solicitation.** During the term of this Agreement and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, neither party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or hire any employee of the other party.

**10. Indemnification.** Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party from any losses arising from its breach of this Agreement.

**11. Residual Knowledge.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts either party from using the general ideas, concepts, know-how or techniques retained in the unaided memory of its personnel who had access to Confidential Information.

**12. Remedies.** Each party acknowledges that a breach may cause irreparable harm, and the non-breaching party may seek injunctive relief without posting a bond.

**13. Compliance Audits.** Upon five (5) business days' notice, the Disclosing Party may audit the Receiving Party's facilities and systems to verify compliance with this Agreement.

**14. No License; As Is.** All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS." No license under any patent, copyright or other right is granted by this Agreement.

**15. Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

**16. Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New York County.

**17. Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

COPPERFIN ROBOTICS, INC. / HALVARD ANALYTICS LLC
