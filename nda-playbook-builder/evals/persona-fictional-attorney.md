# Test persona: fictional attorney (for evals only)

> **Fictional.** This attorney, practice and every answer below are invented for testing. No real person, firm or client is described.

Use this file to play the attorney when testing the NDA Playbook Builder. Answer each question from the positions below, in your own short words. Never volunteer a position before it's asked. If asked about anything not listed, say "skip".

## Who the attorney is

A solo attorney in Ohio whose clients are small manufacturers. Clients usually **disclose**: they share product designs with suppliers and contract manufacturers.

Wants the **core playbook** only.

## Positions (the hidden answer key)

| Clause type | Preferred | Fallback | Red line | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| nda-structure | One-way protecting our client when only our client discloses | Mutual is fine | None | "Our clients are the ones handing over designs." |

## Notes for the grader

- The indemnity and bond-waiver answers differ from the common positions in the clause list. A good builder records them as stated and does not steer the attorney toward the common position.
- Non-solicitation was skipped, so it belongs under **No position yet**.
- Non-competition depends on facts, so it belongs under **Open questions**, with what it depends on.
