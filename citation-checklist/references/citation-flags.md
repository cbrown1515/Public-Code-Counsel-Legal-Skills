# Citation flags

The flags the Citation Checklist applies. Each one is something the document itself shows. None of them says whether an authority exists, says what the document claims, or is still good law. Only a person checking the source can say that.

Apply a flag only when its test is met. When in doubt, don't flag; list the row and let the attorney check it.

| Flag | Applies to | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **QUOTE** | quotations | The document puts words in quotation marks and attributes them to an authority. Every one goes in the quotations table, because each must be checked word for word. |
| **NO-PIN** | quotations and specific holdings | A quotation, or a sentence stating what a case held or a rule requires, cites a case without a pinpoint page or paragraph. Not for "see generally" or a citation to a whole statute section. |
| **NO-ANTECEDENT** | short forms | A short form (a short case name with "at", *Id.*, *supra*, or "the [case name] court") appears before, or without, any full citation of that authority in the document. |
| **ID-AMBIGUOUS** | *Id.* | *Id.* follows a citation sentence that cites more than one authority, or another authority was cited between the one it seems to mean and the *Id.* |
| **INCONSISTENT** | authorities cited more than once | The same authority appears with a different case name spelling, volume, reporter, first page, court or year in different places. Flag every version and list each place. |
| **INCOMPLETE** | full citations | A full case citation lacks a volume, reporter, first page or year. A statute or rule citation lacks its section or rule number. |
| **PIN-BEFORE-FIRST** | pinpoints | A pinpoint page is lower than the case's first page. |
| **FUTURE-DATE** | full citations | The year in a citation is later than the document's date. Use the date the user gives, or the date on the document. If there is neither, skip this check and say so under Not checked. |
| **RECORD** | record citations | A citation to the record, an appendix or an exhibit (for example "CR 214", "R. 33", "App. 12"). These can only be checked against the record, so list them with their type. |

## Words the checklist never uses about an authority

verified, valid, real, fake, nonexistent, fabricated, good law, bad law, overruled, correct citation.

The checklist describes what the document shows and what a person must check. It never states a conclusion about the authority itself.
