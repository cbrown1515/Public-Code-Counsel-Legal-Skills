# Texas Title Examination Standards: add your copy

This skill treats the **Texas Title Examination Standards** as its primary authority. The Real Estate, Probate and Trust Law Section of the State Bar of Texas adopts them; they appear in Vernon's Texas Statutes and Codes Annotated (Property Code, Title 2, Appendix), and REPTL links to them from its site: https://reptl.org/texas-title-examination-standards.

The open-source copy of this skill doesn't include the standards' text. To use the skill at full strength:

1. Get the current standards from REPTL's page or your copy of Vernon's.
2. Paste them into this file in place of this note, keeping the chapter and standard numbers, and put the edition date at the top.
3. If your tool limits file sizes, keep only the chapters your practice uses.

Until you add them, the skill cites standards by number and asks the attorney to confirm each one in their copy.
